## Question

Rating threshold to unlock, and what the next hotel adds: more floors, more languages, guest types, faster spawn, higher fares.

Also settle: can a new hotel's elevator come with music built in (see the upgrades ticket)?

## Resolution

**Hotel ladder (full game for now; must be data-driven so more hotels can be added later):**

| # | Hotel | Floors | Languages | Signature twist |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Rustic cabin** (start) | 3 | English only (assumption: an easy start; the user said "maybe as start") | calm, few guests |
| 1 | Pension Bellevue | 7 | + Spanish | first foreign guests |
| 2 | Grand Hotel | 12 | + French | |
| 3 | Business Tower | 20 | + German, Japanese | guests in a hurry, little patience |
| 4 | Royal Palace | 30 | all | VIPs: big tips, quick complaints |

- **Unlock:** average ≥ 4 stars over the last 3 shifts **and** a one-off application fee (≈ €500, scaled per hotel).
- **Harder and richer:** more floors, more guests at once, less patience, a new language; fares and tips × 1.5 per hotel.
- **No going back** to earlier hotels (maybe in the future).
- **Music:** each new elevator arrives silent; your own music box comes with you and you install it in the new cab.
- **Guest types:** one signature type per hotel; the details belong to guest variety.
