## Question

Define one shift: how guests spawn on floors, the call/queue system, greet → chat → floor selection → ride → drop-off, what ends a shift, and the money formula (base fare, speed bonus, tip for correct/polite service, penalties for wrong floor or waiting too long).

## Resolution

- **Shift:** one in-game day, 08:00–18:00, about 8–10 real minutes, with a lunch rush. Guests spawn on random floors; the shift ends when the clock runs out.
- **Calls:** a call panel in the cab shows waiting floors, with a ding. The player picks the order; impatient guests' lights blink.
- **Controls:** everything through physical buttons in first person: floor buttons, door open/close, and facing a guest to talk. The elevator never moves on its own.
- **Capacity:** several guests per ride with different floors. Starts at 3, raised by an upgrade.
- **Money:** a small base fare plus a tip (up to 3× the fare) based on wait time (call → board), right floor and friendliness in the chat. Wrong floor: no tip and a grumpy guest. Waiting too long: the guest leaves and pays nothing.
- **Rating:** hotel stars drop with complaints. Too low: fired, retry the shift. High rating unlocks the next hotel. Money pays for upgrades.
- **Music:** better music (an upgrade) makes guests more patient during the ride.
