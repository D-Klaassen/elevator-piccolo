## Question

Lock the slice content list: number of floors, guest count, languages (1 known + 1 foreign?), which 2–3 upgrades, length of play, success criteria.

Note: the hotel ladder now starts at a 3-floor rustic cabin (English only), with Spanish arriving at Pension Bellevue. Decide which hotel(s) the demo contains, and check upgrade prices against the guest numbers there.

## Resolution

- **Hotels:** rustic cabin (3 floors, English only) + Pension Bellevue (7 floors, Spanish guests), including the unlock (4★ + application fee).
- **Upgrades:** music (3 levels, starts silent), Spanish lessons (all 5), bigger cab, faster doors. Faster motor and call panel+ come after the demo.
- **Length:** 45–60 minutes: 2–3 shifts in the cabin, 5–7 in Pension Bellevue, ending around lesson 3 with a "to be continued: Grand Hotel" screen.
- **Cabin economy:** ~15 guests per shift (≈ €40 for a beginner); cabin upgrade prices are half the catalogue, Pension Bellevue uses the catalogue as written.
- **Art:** store-bought low-poly packs (e.g. Synty, Kenney) for the cab, hallways and 4–6 guest models with simple animations (wave, point, mime). No custom art.
- **Dialogue:** English guests greet you and you pick 2–3 replies (friendly / neutral / rude), which affects the tip. Spanish guests work as in the language-guessing test.
- **Tech:** save between shifts, basic settings (volume, mouse sensitivity), controller support for Steam Deck. No Steam integration yet; builds for Windows, macOS and Steam Deck.
- **Success:** 3–5 friends play without explanation, get the loop within 5 minutes and want "one more shift"; 60 fps on Steam Deck.

**Update 2026-10-21:** the demo targets Windows and macOS for now, 60 fps on a typical Mac or Windows PC. The Steam Deck comes later: there's no Deck to test on. Controller support stays.
