## Question

Options for commercially-licensed lounge/elevator music: royalty-free libraries, CC0, commissioning, generating; licensing terms for a paid Steam game; how dynamic music (per upgrade/hotel) could work.

## Resolution

**Prototype with CC0 tracks (Pixabay as fallback). Commission a composer for the shipped game:** loopable lounge tracks delivered as stems, with full or exclusive rights including soundtrack DLC. Stems let music layer per upgrade or hotel. Log where every prototype track came from. Pixabay forbids selling its tracks unchanged, so no DLC from Pixabay tracks. Avoid AI music as the main score (unclear copyright, Steam disclosure). Kevin MacLeod tracks need credit and are overused. Not checked: subscription libraries such as Epidemic Sound.
